# Privacy Policy – FlowBlock AI

**Last updated:** July 26, 2026

FlowBlock AI does not collect, transmit, or sell any user data.

The extension itself does not send any telemetry, error reports, crash reports, usage statistics, or other data to the creators.

- All ad/tracker blocking, pattern learning, and analysis happen entirely on your device.
- Settings, learned rules, whitelist entries, and repair history are stored only locally in your browser.
- No browsing history, page content, or personal information is sent to any server.
- The on-device AI model is downloaded once to your device and then runs locally. No data is sent to the model provider during use.

We do not use analytics, tracking, or third-party data services inside the extension.

Any install counts or aggregate usage information visible to the developer come only from the Chrome Web Store dashboard provided by Google, not from the extension.
